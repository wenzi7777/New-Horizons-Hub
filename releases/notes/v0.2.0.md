# v0.2.0

## Changes

### New: ESP-NOW OTA relay for paired devices with no WiFi of their own

- A device paired over ESP-NOW (New Horizons Direct) had no way to fetch
  its own OTA manifest or firmware -- it has no WiFi/internet access at
  all. The Hub now does this on its behalf over two new ESP-NOW channels:
  a small device-initiated request/reply channel (`kEspNowFragTypeHubRequest`,
  new `EspNowHubManager` demux + per-slot reassembler) for asking the Hub
  to fetch a manifest URL and forward the raw JSON back, and a Hub-pushed
  chunk channel (`kEspNowFragTypeOta`, new `EspNowOtaRelay` class) for
  streaming the firmware binary itself once the device decides it needs
  the update.
- Deliberately global single-session, not per-device: only one device's
  relay may be active Hub-wide at a time, bounding the Hub's extra
  resource cost (one HTTP connection, one chunk buffer) to a fixed
  constant regardless of roster size -- other devices' sensor-data polling
  is completely unaffected by an in-progress relay.
- The Hub never touches `Update.*` for this -- it's a pure byte relay; the
  device is solely responsible for writing flash and verifying SHA256 (see
  the NewHorizonsOS-OTA v0.13.0 release, which this pairs with).
- Real-hardware validated end-to-end: relayed a real ~1.4MB firmware image
  (363 chunks) to a real device, which applied it and rebooted
  successfully with a matching SHA256.
- **Bug found and fixed during that validation**: `EspNowOtaRelay` was
  sending all ~16 ESP-NOW fragments of a chunk back-to-back in one
  unpaced burst, which silently drops most fragments on real hardware
  (`ESP_ERR_ESPNOW_NO_MEM`) -- the same class of bug this codebase has
  now hit and fixed three times (previously in `EspNowStreamTransport` and
  `EspNowPairing`'s command-response path). Fixed by pacing fragment sends
  across `service()` ticks the same way those two already do. Before the
  fix a 363-chunk relay took 17+ minutes and was still running; after the
  fix chunks progressed steadily without stalling.
- Known limitation, not addressed this round: relay throughput is still
  much slower than a rough estimate when the device is concurrently
  streaming sensor data at full fps, since both share the same ESP-NOW
  airtime -- not a functional defect, just a real bandwidth constraint,
  documented for now.

### New: quick-power-cycle factory reset (no physical button needed)

- The current GCU V2.3.D board has no physical setup/reset button (a
  future board revision adds one). Power the Hub off and back on 5 times
  in a row, each cycle within about 5 seconds of the previous power-on,
  and it factory-resets and boots into the SoftAP setup portal -- the same
  outcome as the portal's own Factory Reset button, reachable even when
  Wi-Fi credentials are wrong or the Backend is unreachable.
- Tracked via a boot counter persisted in NVS (survives real power loss,
  unlike RTC memory), incremented once per boot and cleared once the Hub
  has stayed powered long enough that the boot clearly wasn't part of a
  quick-cycle sequence.
- Real-hardware validated: 5 quick resets correctly triggered the reset
  and the Hub came up in SoftAP setup mode.
